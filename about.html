I started Digital Health Pulse to keep track of the health tech startups I kept reading about, and to get better at telling which ones will last.

I study Economics at UCLA, and next summer I'm joining L.E.K. Consulting as a Summer Associate, focused on life sciences and healthcare. Once a month I add new companies to the tracker, mostly in AI diagnostics and tech-driven drug discovery, with my notes on how each one plans to make money and what could get in its way.

How does better data change decision-making in healthcare, and who controls that process?

A lot of what gets written about this space is hype. I try to look past the funding headline and ask whether the company solves a problem someone will actually pay for, which is a surprisingly good filter.

I'm always up for a conversation, whether you're a student getting into the field or someone who's spent years in it. The easiest way to reach me is by email or LinkedIn.
